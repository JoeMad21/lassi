## Scope

P11 builds what the P11 row of the bible's Build Roadmap names (verbatim): "df dialect in ODS, IRDL export, lowering to ttkernel, recipe target switch". That is the df level of IR Strategy (its table's df row; Dataflow Dialect): the dialect defined in ODS under dialects/df/ (Repository Layout), its IRLevel behind the existing interface (Component Interfaces, IRLevel), its IRDL export for xDSL, its lowering to the ttkernel level P5 builds, and the one-line switch the Dataflow Dialect section describes, with its pins (Toolchain Pins). Out: any stage that asks a model (P6), the csl lowering and xDSL's csl dialects (P13), a df corpus or pair set (a later corpus phase), df's context pack for the stages that ask a model (P6 builds it from P11.5's generated docs, the one file that serves people and model context, Readability Standards, Dialect docs), a conversion into df from cpu-mlir or a lift from ttkernel unless OQ-061 places one here, and a first non-Tenstorrent target unless OQ-060 places one here.

Gate (the P11 row's Gate column, verbatim): "Tier A pairs round-trip through df". It names no device and no owner review, so a pass sets P11 DONE; it runs on ttsim, a simulator (Agent Rule 2). Relation to other phases: df's ttkernel output goes through P5's ttkernel level and Tenstorrent target (P5.6, P5.9, P5.10), and its round trips extend P5.12's driver, so P11.5, and through it every task after the design notes, waits for P5's gate (P5.G), as AGENTS.md, Work Order, says ("P11 beyond design notes needs P5"). That chain holds P5's cgeist work, though P11's gate under OQ-061 (a) raises no C++: P5.G needs P5.12, which needs P5.7 (cgeist, after P5.5's Polygeist LLVM build) and goes OWNER if a Tier A C++ reference is beyond cgeist's reach (plans/p5-ir.md, P5.7), and P11.6 needs P5.6, which needs P5.5. P12 runs beside it (AGENTS.md, Work Order); P13 lowers df to CSL through the IRDL export; P6 binds df in the lassi-df recipe's MLIR-level stages.
