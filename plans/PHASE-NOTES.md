- Compiler output facts (P0.15 captures; tests/toolchains/fixtures/README.md): nvc++ EDG lines read `"main.cpp", line 7: error: ...` (warnings end in ` [tag]`, errors carry none) and nvcc EDG lines read `main.cu(7): error: ...` (errors carry no number, warnings `#177-D`); each nvcc EDG diagnostic appears once. -Minfo goes to stderr after the EDG lines and before the link lines, and in every capture nvc++ numbers a target region by the line before its pragma (there, the function's opening brace). Exit statuses: nvcc 1 (driver fatal, link), 2 (EDG error), 255 (ptxas); nvc++ 2 for every error class; warnings alone exit 0.
