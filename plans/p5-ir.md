## Scope

P5 builds what the P5 row of the bible's Build Roadmap names (verbatim): "C and C++ frontends (cgeist), Tenstorrent target, TT raiser, normalize, execution gate; v0 corpus migrated into `lassi/corpus/`". That is the cpu-mlir and ttkernel levels of IR Strategy with their raise and lower paths, the llvm-ir level the CPU lowering passes through, normalize and the execution gate of the Corpus Pipeline, and the migration the Review Of v0 table asks for. The Frontend, Target, and IRLevel Protocols exist in lassi/core/interfaces.py since P0 with no implementation, and Trial.toolchain_pins already has llvm, polygeist, and tt_mlir fields (lassi/core/record.py); P5 gives them their first components and values. It pins and installs Polygeist and tt-mlir (Toolchain Pins; tt-mlir at the joint pin P4.1 chose), and builds Tier B of tt-pairs-v0, which the gate names and the bible lists as planned, not built (Benchmark Suites, TT tiers), and takes it through the round trip.

Gate (the P5 row's Gate column, verbatim): "Every Tier A and B reference pair round-trips on ttsim; per-kernel gate rates reported". It runs as P5.G and names no owner review, so a pass sets P5 DONE.

Relation to other phases: P6 binds these components in the lassi-df recipe's MLIR-level stages (Project Recipes) and adds the MLIR prompt set and context packs; P5 adds no stage that asks a model, and its driver is `lassi corpus`. P11 lowers df to ttkernel through P5's ttkernel level and gates on Tier A round trips through df with P5's driver; its design notes (PHASE-NOTES P11) may go to the owner queue in parallel and are not P5 tasks. P12 adds the other frontends behind the same Frontend interface and hub level. P13 and P14 are untouched.
