  gpt-oss-120b at least about 64K with its 32768; if either is served shorter, the job is stopped and relaunched with
  smaller blocks (OQ-086's autonomy). run.sh waits up to 1200 s for each server to list its model; a slower first
  load of a model never served on this host is logged as FAIL, and the remedy is the same relaunch.
- At the end: tables7 (tabulate.py), the per-trial feature counts (count_features.py), and the classifier's report,
  pulled into results/ with provenance.
