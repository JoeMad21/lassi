## The Plan

One big job, started at 20:00 from a clean commit in its own slot, which needs no session once started; its rx
timeout ends it at 02:00, and its deadline is 01:45 EDT:

```
PYTHONIOENCODING=utf-8 uv run tools/rx.py job start --big --slot night --name night --timeout <seconds to 02:00> -- \
  'bash plans/runs/night/run-night.sh <01:45 EDT as epoch seconds>'
```

- Downloads: Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct-FP8-dynamic (73.8 GB) before the sweep, gpt-oss-120b (198.6 GB) in the
  background (the sums of every file in https://huggingface.co/api/models/furiosa-ai/<model>/tree/v2026.4?recursive=1,
  read 2026-10-25), each every file of its v2026.4 revision, each only with the 60 GB floor and a 5 GB margin kept
  after it. The afternoon's cleanup left 346 GB free (rx 20261025-133317-exec-489c), so after Llama about 272 GB
  remain against gpt-oss-120b's 264 GB need: an 8 GB margin. The scratch root then grows past the 150G cap, under the
  owner's lift (OQ-074) and OQ-087's choice.
- Assets built on the host and checked against their tracked manifests: the full API pack (all 52 headers), the
  worked examples (tt-examples-v0: shard_data_rm and add_2_integers_in_riscv), and the docs index.
- The sweep over projects/lassi-demo/sweep7, run.sh once per row of models10.tsv on port 8123: Llama until the
  night's midpoint, then gpt-oss-120b, once its download has ended, until the deadline. Per model, at both tolerance
  halves: base (no feature but the fuzzer), ex (the worked examples before generation and compile corrections), lk (the full pack's declarations: the core host API
  headers at generation, and at compile corrections the declarations the errors name with up to 3 nearest declared
  names), and all (ex, lk, retrieval, the mismatch report, restarts after 3); gpt-oss-120b also runs all at low
  effort (lowall). Llama's budget is 8192 tokens; gpt-oss-120b's 32768.
- First check after each model is served: its max_model_len in the server's model list (run.sh's
  <slug>.models.json). PROJECTED, at an assumed 3.5 characters per token: the largest generation prompt is about
  20K tokens (about 68,500 characters: examples, declarations, passages, template, and source), and a correction
  adds the candidate's files and errors, so Llama needs at least about 32K of context with its 8192-token budget and
  gpt-oss-120b at least about 64K with its 32768; if either is served shorter, the job is stopped and relaunched with
  smaller blocks (OQ-086's autonomy). run.sh waits up to 1200 s for each server to list its model; a slower first
  load of a model never served on this host is logged as FAIL, and the remedy is the same relaunch.
- At the end: tables7 (tabulate.py), the per-trial feature counts (count_features.py), and the classifier's report,
  pulled into results/ with provenance.
